About this score

FIMD is an experimental composite, not an official INSEE index. This first build is income-focused: poverty 40%, low median income 35%, minima sociaux 10%, housing benefits 10%, unemployment-income share 5%.

Primary data: INSEE Filosofi 2021 where available; fallback: Filosofi 2020. IRIS geometry: IGN/INSEE via Esri France.
